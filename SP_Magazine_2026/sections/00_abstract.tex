AI agents triage alerts in Security Operations Centres (SOCs), yet
their failure conditions are poorly understood. \ARES{} models agent
failures as skill, knowledge, and capability mismatches. Using
\ARESBench{} and \SAFK{}, we show knowledge mismatch drives
hallucination, mismatch dimensions produce distinct failure
signatures, and multi-agent pipelines amplify reliability risk.
